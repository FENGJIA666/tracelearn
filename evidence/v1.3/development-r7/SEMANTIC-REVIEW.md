# Complete development semantic review

**Codex-assisted review by the dataset-author agent. The review is not blinded or independent human validation, and it is not evidence of learning gains or competition scores.**

All 40 development cases and both configurations were reviewed: 80 logical trials across four fictional materials. This split was available for tuning; no holdout output was read. Raw responses, errors, rejected attempts and per-claim rationale remain available.

The comparison is previous Qwen3:4b production configuration versus candidate Qwen3.5:9b local configuration. Model weights and production settings differ; the comparison isolates neither citation review nor model weights. The manifest locks the actual model digests and settings.

The previous configuration had 10 runtime errors. The candidate had 2 unverified, withheld outcomes, which are no-answer cases rather than correct refusals. All failures remain in the denominators. Latency includes internal retries and failed trials under the recorded alternating-variant sequence; these are not controlled cold-start measurements. The JSON gives outcome-specific latency, all failure counts and raw-line fingerprints.

Paired outcomes: 15 cases were correct only in the candidate, 1 only in the baseline, 19 in both and 5 in neither. These development differences are not a generalization or causal estimate.
